_This is a reply to Justin Michael's excellent article titled: ["We Need Smart People Who Give A Shit"](https://violetpixel.com/blog/2026-11-17-we-need-smart-people-who-give-a-shit/)_

In short, Justin makes the case that as more and more complicated systems are being run by AI, we are losing the people who are intimately connected to those systems in both their knowledge of said systems AND their perpensity to actually care about how those systems work.  

I think this quote sums it up perfectly (and as a software engineer by trade, I can confirm that this is 100% accurate):

>Telling people to stop doing the job they signed up for in favor of babysitting LLMs so they can do that job instead is a great way to make people less smart about the system they used to work on and give less of a shit about it.

Justin's article centers on technology systems and the impact AI has had on them.  But I think the same problem exists in government (at least here in the U.S.A. where I live.)  It seems like politicians are interested in pushing some specific agenda instead of serving in the best interest of the public. 

We need people who understand how our government is meant to function and actually give a shit about democracy and making it work.  

The problem here isn't AI but rather corruption and the culture war we have found ourselves in recently.  But, much like AI, the root of the problem is the late stage of capitalism that we're currently living in.
