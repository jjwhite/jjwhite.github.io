Prior to being invited to this show by a friend of mine, I had never heard of [Fruition](https://www.fruitionband.com/).  I'd also never been to Milkboy Philly and didn't really know what to expect.  I spent some time the week prior listeing to Fruition's catalog and thought it was quite good.

# The Venue

[Milkboy Philly](https://milkboyphilly.com/) is located right in center city Philadelphia at 11th and Chestnut.  Downstairs it's a bar and restaurant.  The night of the show was an unseasonably warm November evening so tables spilled out of the open garage-style doors where patrons were enjoying the mild weather.  The concert venue is upstairs where there is another bar, a stage, and standing room only. 


# The Openers

The openers for the show were [Handmade Moments](https://www.handmademomentsmusic.net/), an eclectic duo out of New Orleans whose energy was encapsulated by frontwoman Anna Moss' bouncing head of curls as she worked through an array of instruments from saxophone to bass clarinet and even beat boxing.  As co-band member Joel Ludford quipped: "Who needs a drummer?"

# The Headliners

As I stood with beer in hand waiting for Fruition to make their appearance on stage out of some hidden side door I felt a tap on my shoulder and heard "band coming through!" Stepping aside I watch as the band, some with instruments strapped to their backs, snake their way through the crowd up to the stage.  Not something I'd ever experienced before.

The band proceeded to play a mix of rock, folk and American roots. Often switching instruments and even places on stage as three of the five members rotated the role of front man (or woman).  You could tell that this is a band that has been playing together for a long time.  At one point, lead guitarist Jay Cobb Anderson broke a string and managed to replace it himself in a matter of a few seconds while the band played on without missing a beat. 

Throughout the set the crowd sang, danced, and sweat along with the band and everyone (myself included) loved every minute of it.  If you get a chance to see either band I seriously doubt you'll be disappointed.
