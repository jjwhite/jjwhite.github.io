_This is a reply to Justin Michael's excellent article titled ["We Need Smart People Who Give A Shit"](https://violetpixel.com/blog/2026-11-17-we-need-smart-people-who-give-a-shit/)._

In short, Justin makes the case that as more and more complicated systems are run by AI, we are losing the people who are intimately connected to those systems, both in their knowledge of the systems and in their propensity to actually care about how those systems work.

I think this quote sums it up perfectly (and as a software engineer by trade, I can confirm that this is my reality):

> Telling people to stop doing the job they signed up for in favor of babysitting LLMs so they can do that job instead is a great way to make people less smart about the system they used to work on and give less of a shit about it.

Justin's article centers on technology systems and the impact AI has had on the people maintaining them. But I think the same problem exists in government (at least here in the U.S.). It seems like politicians are more interested in pushing a specific agenda than in serving the best interest of the public.

Justin ends his piece by saying, "I think what we have here is a hot stove moment for capitalism..." I think he's correct, but we're also in a hot stove moment for democracy.

We need people who understand how our government is meant to function and who actually give a shit about democracy and making it work.
