 Remember when the internet was made for people by people and they syndicated their content via RSS?  Well, Pepperidge Farm remembers, and so do I. [Enshittification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification) is absolutely a real thing and it's making the internet nearly unusable. Add social media algorithms and the fact that sites are now starting to cater their content to AI agents and it's becoming nearly impossible to just read what you want on the web these days.

Sure, RSS isn't exactly _dead_ but every modern RSS reader feels like it's trying too hard.  So, I thought I'd vibe code one for myself that does exactly what an RSS reader should do and nothing more.  No account, no database, just json data that you can export or import or sync with dropbox or another file sharing service.  


Try it out now [here](https://jonwhite.me/reader)
