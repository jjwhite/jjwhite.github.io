### Scales
Scales always follow the same pattern of whole(W) and half(H) steps:

*W-W-H-W-W-W-H*

in guitar frets this translates to:

*2-2-1-2-2-2-1*

So, if you want to play any given scale on a single guitar string, just pick a starting note and then follow that pattern.

*Examples:*

Scale of G:

*G-A-B-C-D-E-F#-G*

Scale of A:

*A-B-C#-D-E-F#-G#*

### Intervals

Intervals are just the gaps between notes. Starting at any given note you can play intervals on the same string by moving up X number of frets:

Major 2nd: 2 frets

Minor second: 1 fret

Major 3rd: 4 frets

Perfect 4th: 5 frets

Perfect 5th: 7 frets

Octave: 12 frets

A perfect 5th can be played on the next string up by moving 2 frets up on that string (unless you’re root is the G string then it’s 3 strings up) (Playing a note plus it’s perfect 5th on the next string are called *power chords*)
